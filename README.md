# My Favorite Anime Series

## The ramparts of Ice

**Genre:** Shonen manga, Romantic comedy, School story

**Created by:** Tea Agasawa

### About the Series
High school student Koyuki Hikawa has trouble interacting with others, instead preferring to keep her distance and spend her time alone. However, when she meets Minato Amamiya, he suddenly starts trying to close the distance between them.

### Why I Like It
- Interesting and exciting storyline.
- Strong friendship between the characters.
- A mature focus on emotional boundaries and learning how to open up after past trauma.
- Realistically flawed, relatable characters who feel like actual teenagers rather than anime tropes.

### My Favorite Character
My favorite character is Koyuki Hikawa because i can somehow relate to his past which is introvert and want to be alone everytime.
